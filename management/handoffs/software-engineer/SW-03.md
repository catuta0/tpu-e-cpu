**Selected: C0.**

How decisive the selection is (information only, not part of the protocol; paired exact McNemar against C0):
- C3 and C4 differ from C0 by small discordant counts (C0-only / other-only from 1/0 to 6/1), every p ≥ 0.125. The protocol's choice among the rule-compliant candidates is not statistically resolved.
- C1 (rev B) at K = 9: 11/0 discordant, p = 0.001. This independently reproduces the SW-02 rev B defect on test-independent data.

C3 and C4 were designed from CALCULATION alone, before any accuracy was computed:
- They come from an exhaustive scan over α of X_i = S(α i), W_i = S(α (15 − i)): 65 288 feasible α out of 65 534.
- C3 is the optimum of (λ8, λ1) and C4 the optimum of (λ1, λ8).
- Maximising the stream-cycle lag λ8 alone pushes the 16 generators into a few bit-shifted phases: C3 has λ1 = 1, so two generators share bits at a lag of 1–2 cycles.

**Finding: R2, stricter than the manager's rule** (CALCULATION, verified against the real LFSR states in `test_shared_bits_window_model_matches_lfsr_states`).
- r is an 8-bit window of one bit sequence. Two generators therefore share bits whenever their phase offset is within ±7 steps of 8·lag, not only at the exact-copy lag.
- Two lanes' products become second-order correlated if both operands share bits at the same lag. This can happen directly or swapped (X_i~W_j with W_i~X_j, including i = j).
- With D_i = n_w(i) − n_x(i) in single steps, the condition is |D_i − D_j| ≤ 14 or |D_i + D_j| ≤ 14.
- R2 forbids both. R1 (the manager's rule) is the exact special case of the direct condition.
- Within the rev B 4096-step lattice, R2 is unsatisfiable: with 8 lanes some pair always has opposite offsets. This is why C2 fails R2.

## Open decisions (for the manager)
- **D-A. Accept "rev C = rev A mode-2 seeds".** The protocol picks C0 by 2 images over C3, which is not significant. The alternatives C3 and C4 are rule-compliant with larger lags.
- **D-B. Modes 0/1 pair.** The rev B/C pair (0xACE1, 0xF9DE) has D = 32768, half the period. It therefore violates the stricter R2 by self-swap: at lag 4096, X(k+4096) = W(k) exactly and W(k+4096) shares 7 bits with X(k). This lag lies inside layer 1 only at K = 10. There is no measured effect: the modes 0/1 ensemble RMS ratio at K = 10 is 0.81–0.91. The rev A pair (0xACE1, 0x7A5C) satisfies R2, but it is a change I was not asked to make. I recommend keeping the pair.
- **D-C. Adopt R2 as a spec rationale.** R2 is what actually excludes second-order coupling; R1 alone accepts C2. The manager's choice.
