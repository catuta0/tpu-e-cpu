Root cause of the rev B K=9 degradation (CALCULATION, proven in `test_compare_revs.py`):
1. X_i and W_i are both spaced 4096 steps per lane, so the joint pair stream of lane i+1 is the pair stream of lane i delayed by 512 stream cycles (1 distinct pair offset out of 8; rev A has 8 of 8).
2. 512 cycles are a whole number of tiles for K ≤ 9, and a K=10 tile is two 512-cycle blocks. In those cases whole XNOR count tables repeat across (tile, lane) slots: at K=9, slot (t, i+1) is identical to slot (t+1, i).
3. Identical slot pairs per inference at K = 6/7/8/9/10: 14/54/120/196/100, out of 3160. Rev A has 0.
4. The errors add coherently. Layer-1 ensemble RMS divided by the eq. (2) prediction: rev B 1.06 / 1.30 / 1.55 at K = 8 / 9 / 10; rev A 0.90–1.01.

## Assumptions
- **Revision tag.** The tag is written `# spec_rev B`, not `// spec_rev B`, because the vector and trace formats use `#` comments, and the README reader and `tests/tpu/tb_sc_tpu.v` peek for `#`. A `//` line would break both readers.
- **Init files carry no tag.** The `$readmemh` init files get no header line, for three reasons: §5.6 fixes their line counts; `tests/tpu/gen_tpu_sweep_vectors.py` (fpga-engineer) parsed them without comment support at the start of this task; and they are identical across revisions.
- **Ensemble method.** The ensemble shifts every LFSR of a revision by the same offset, which keeps that revision's relative phases. It is the right null distribution for "same seed rule, another start point".
- **Statistics.** Newcombe method 10 for the paired CI; two-sided exact binomial for McNemar; no correction for multiple comparisons outside the 2 pre-specified cells.
